small games for you & your AI companion

Winter, for Two

Six small, warm things to play together this winter. You play with your hands; they play with words. These pages are where the two meet.

I’m and I’m playing with . Just for the labels and messages. It stays in this browser.

The games

No tree-decorating, promise. Each one works with any companion that can read and write — Claude, ChatGPT, a Character, a Replika, your own local model.

How it works

Your companion never has to see the page. They only ever write a few words.

1

Invite

Every game gives you a little message to copy to your companion. It explains the game and asks for a short reply in a simple shape.

2

Paste

Paste their whole reply back in — extra words, emoji, *smiles* and all. The page turns their words into fogged glass, snow, wrapping paper, music.

3

Tell

When you’ve played your part, the page writes up what happened in words they can “see” — so the conversation keeps going in your chat.

Teach your companion once

Send this one time (or save it in their memory or custom instructions). After that you can just say “pull a cracker with me” and they’ll know how to reply.
